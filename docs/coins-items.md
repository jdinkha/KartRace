# Coins, items and payouts

Read before changing pickups, coins, items, crash penalties, payouts or anything that saves coins (e.g. a shop).

- Coins and items (`PICKUPS` table in `RaceServer`; the client copies `MAX_COINS`, `COIN_SPEED` and `ITEM_BOOST_TIME` in `HANDLING`, so keep them the same):
  - `buildPickups` places them on every track by itself, clear of gaps, ramps/banners (`route.busy`) and hazards. Coins come in lines of 4 that swing from side to side down the main road, plus one line on each branch. Item boxes come in rows right across every road (`itemRows`). **Rule: whichever way you go, you pass an item row about every 10 s, from the start to the finish.** On the main road the first is `FIRST_ITEM_ROW` (200) studs after the start line (~3 s in, so there's an item in the first 10 s even where a shortcut leaves the main road early, like Magma's and Moonlight Manor's), then one every `ITEM_ROW_SPACING` (800) studs, about every 10 s of driving. That includes main road a branch or drop bypasses (rows used to skip it, which left Moonlight Manor's main road ~20 s between items). Each branch gets round(length ÷ 800) rows spread evenly along it, so none on one under ~400 studs. Rows keep off jump run-ups and hazards (the road's `jumpZones`, `hazardZones`) and stay 200 studs from any other row on that road; where a row can't go, it moves on 25 studs at a time to the next good spot, and the next row is spaced from there. A road with hazards from end to end gets none (Magma's Lava Tube, Croaker's High Passage). A track can add more on any road with a `Coins` feature (a line of coins) or an `Items` feature (a row of item boxes across the road): the Wine Racks uses both to make its longer route worth taking.
  - The server picks them up. Each frame it sweeps each kart's path since the last frame (`REACH`); bots collect too. A grabbed pickup is unparented and comes back after `RESPAWN_TIME` (1.5 s). The client spins them, plays the sounds and animates them from the `Pickups` folder's `ChildRemoved` (a fading copy flies up or bursts, with sparkles, and a sound from that spot) and `ChildAdded` (grows back in with a little bounce).
  - The server's word arrives a moment late, so the client predicts its own grabs. It sweeps its kart's path each frame with `PICKUP_REACH`, kept a bit under the server's `REACH`. When it hits one, it hides it locally (`LocalTransparencyModifier`) and bursts it at once with the sound in your ears, and remembers it in `predicted`. When the server later removes that pickup, it doesn't burst again. If the server never takes it (1.5 s), it pops back. The HUD coin count adds predicted coins (`shownCoins`). The counts that matter (coins, items, speed) still come from the server.
  - Item boxes show "?" with a SurfaceGui on every face. Don't go back to one BillboardGui inside the see-through box: it flickers.
  - Coins: up to `MAX_COINS` (20) per race, reset to 0 every race. Each coin carried adds `COIN_SPEED` (0.85%) top speed (players and bots).
  - The client's "use item" wait (`itemPending`) clears when your item changes, or after 1 s at most: stealing a Stealer leaves the item the same, and the wait used to get stuck.
  - Speed boosts add up, they don't multiply: top speed = `MAX_SPEED` (83.2) × (1 + 0.85% per coin (a coin a Taxman just took counts `TAX_BOOST` (5) times) + boost bonus), where the boost bonus is what `BOOST_SPEED` (119.6) is over `MAX_SPEED` (+44%). `MAX_SPEED`/`BOOST_SPEED` went up from 80/115 when `COIN_SPEED` dropped from 1% to 0.85%, so 15 coins is still a bit faster than before (93.8 vs 92 studs/s). Same formula for players (`driveStep`) and bots (`driveBot`). Off-road still multiplies (it's a slow-down).
  - Crashing costs coins (`loseCoins`): `CRASH_COINS` (3) for getting hit by a dangerous hazard (`Bonk`/`Launch`, a banana peel) and for every respawn (manual, lava or falling). Not for safe obstacles (crates, rocks, crystals, snowmen, frog idols, trees: they're just walls; see `docs/hazards.md`), walls or bumping karts. At most once per `CRASH_COOLDOWN` (1.5 s). The client notices its own hazard hits and sends `Crash` (it can only cost the sender, so there's nothing to cheat); bots lose them in `knockBot` and `respawnRacer`. The server fires `CoinsLost` to everyone, and each client spills that many coins out of the kart with a red "-3" above it (your coin count flashes red).
  - Items: one held at a time. `PICKUPS.ITEMS` is a table of weights (all 1 = every item equally likely); a box picks with them (`randomItem(true)`), whatever your place. Bots use theirs 1–4 s after getting it (a Mega Jump only on a straight bit of road, not just before a gap). The client shows each one with `ITEM_LOOKS` (name and colour) in the item icon, top left under your coins (Roblox's player list covers the top right). Clicking or tapping the icon uses the item, same as E / X; touch screens have no separate ITEM button.
    - `Boost` and `MegaJump` start on the client the moment you press E (it owns its kart); the server just takes the item away (and does them for bots). Mega Jump throws you up at `MEGA_JUMP` (90 studs/s) with gravity softened to `MEGA_GRAVITY` (0.45 of normal; other jumps use `AIR_GRAVITY` 0.55): about 45 studs high and ~2 s in the air. Until you land (`d.flying`) the kart flies like a plane: full steering, drifting (it charges mini-turbos too), gas and brake all work, turning swings your speed round with you instead of losing it, and nothing slows you down (no coasting slow-down, no easing down after a boost). Landing keeps your speed and drift, and the touchdown can't count as a wall hit (`wallGrace`). Bots get the same: full steering (`ai.flying`) and the same gravity. Keep `MEGA_JUMP` and `MEGA_GRAVITY` the same in `PICKUPS` and the client's `HANDLING`; if you change them, keep the height near 45 studs (height = `MEGA_JUMP`² ÷ (2 × 196.2 × `MEGA_GRAVITY`)) or check what karts can now reach (see CLAUDE.md).
    - `TripleCoin`: +3 coins on the server; one coin sound, pitched for the count you end up on.
    - `Banana`: the server drops a peel `BANANA_THROW` (80) studs ahead on the road (none if that's off the road). Its looks are `ServerStorage.BananaPeel`, a MeshPart (a peeled banana standing on its four flaps, ~5 studs across and 4.4 tall, made with Studio's AI mesh generator) cloned standing on the road. It's a model in `Hazards` with a `BananaHit` part (`Hazard = "Banana"`, `Thrower`, `SafeUntil`, `ArmedAt`): armed 0.35 s later, and the thrower can't slip on it for `BANANA_SAFE` (2 s). The first kart to touch it spins out on slippery tyres and loses crash coins; a player's client sends `HitBanana` (the server checks the peel is near their kart) and the peel's removed for everyone. Leftover peels are cleared when the race ends.
    - `Mist` (Inversion Mist): every racer ahead of the user (by `livePlace`) gets `Mist` (who, start, end in server time): a `MIST_WARNING` (2 s) warning while the purple haze thickens from nothing to full, then their steering is reversed for `MIST_TIME` (3 s) under the full haze. Bots ahead weave about instead. Mists stack: the server keeps each racer's Mist (`mistBy`, `mistFrom`, `mistUntil`), and another Mist while one still has you (warning or swapped) adds `MIST_STACK` (2 s) to its end, keeping the first one's name, warning and countdown. The client sees the same `from` again and just extends it (a different `from` starts a new haze).
    - `Stealer`: takes the item of a random racer holding one (skipping the user and finishers; a spinning Roulette can't be stolen). Nobody holding one: it's used up ("Nobody had an item to steal!").
    - `Roulette`: the item becomes `"Rolling"` (can't be used or stolen; the HUD flicks through every item) for `ROULETTE_SPIN` (0.8 s), then a random item picked evenly from all of them, Roulette included, ignoring the weights (`randomItem(false)`).
    - `Taxman`: takes min(`TAX` (5), their coins) from every racer ahead of the user (by `livePlace`) still racing, adds it all to the user, and for `TAX_TIME` (5 s) lets them carry more than `MAX_COINS` (`taxUntil`). In 1st place it takes nothing. For those 5 s every coin it took (`taxCoins`, the player's `TaxCoins` attribute; it shrinks if they drop coins) is worth `TAX_BOOST` (5) coins of speed, even if they end up under 20 (`TAX_BOOST` is in the client's `HANDLING` too); then the boost ends and they drop back to 20 if over. Payout never counts more than `MAX_COINS`.
    - `Teleport`: swaps places with the racer in the place just ahead (5th swaps with 4th; if that racer has finished, the nearest one ahead still racing): kart positions (speeds kept), next checkpoint, respawn point and progress, so their places swap too (`swapPlaces`). A player is moved with `Respawned` (reason `"Teleport"`). In 1st there's nobody ahead: it's used up ("Nobody ahead to swap with!").
    - Item messages reach the player as `ItemNews` (Stole, Robbed, NothingToSteal, Taxed, TaxedBy, Swapped, SwappedBy, NoOneAhead).
    - Test in Studio (Server, mid-race): `game.ServerStorage.GiveItem:Invoke("Player1", "Taxman")` (a player's name or a CPU's, like `"Blaze (CPU)"`).
  - Payout (`payCoins`): finishers are paid as they cross the line, everyone else when the race ends. Amount = coins × 2 / 1.5 / 1.25 for 1st / 2nd / 3rd, rounded up; everyone else ×1. Totals are saved with `IncrementAsync` in the `Coins_v1` DataStore (key = user id) and shown as leaderstats `Coins`. Players who leave mid-race still get theirs saved. Spending coins later (a shop) should use `UpdateAsync` on the same key and refuse to go below 0.
